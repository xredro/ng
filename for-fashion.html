Questions sellers ask

Can I stop customers ordering a piece that is gone?

Yes. Mark any product Sold out in one tap. Customers see it straight away and cannot add it, while the rest of your store keeps selling.

Does X Redro confirm that a transfer actually arrived?

You can upload your bank statement as a text-based PDF and X Redro helps you match each customer's payment to their order. It reads the file in your browser and never uploads it. You stay in control: nothing is marked paid until you confirm. Scanned or photographed statements are not supported.

What if a customer wants to add something after ordering?

After ordering, the customer downloads an order card with an ID. With it they can look up their order, add products or change quantities. Each change waits for your approval, and you can switch this off in your settings.
