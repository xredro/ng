Set up your store and share it

About 1 minute

Pick a starter store, name it, add your bank details, swap the samples for your products, save, then copy your link.

Read the steps instead
  1. Pick what you sell and get a ready store with sample products.
  2. Type your store name.
  3. Replace the [brackets] in the sub heading with your bank details.
  4. Open each sample product and add your own photo, name and price.
  5. Press Save, then copy your store link from the dashboard and share it.

What your customer sees

About 45 seconds

Open the store, add to cart, check out, upload payment proof and get an order card.

Read the steps instead
  1. The customer opens your link and taps Add on what they want.
  2. They open the cart and check out.
  3. They enter the details you ask for.
  4. They pay by bank transfer and upload a picture of the receipt.
  5. They send the order and get an order card with an ID to keep.

Your orders and payment proof

About 40 seconds

See new orders, open one, check the payment proof, mark it Paid and Delivered.

Read the steps instead
  1. New orders appear at the top of your Orders page.
  2. Tap an order to see the customer, what they ordered and their payment proof.
  3. Check the payment, then mark it Paid.
  4. When it is on its way, mark it Delivered.
  5. Use the filters and the search box to find an order quickly.

When a customer changes an order

About 55 seconds

A customer adds an item with their order ID and sends extra proof. You approve it and record the payment.

Read the steps instead
  1. The customer taps Retrieve My Order and types the ID from their order card.
  2. They add a product and see the new total and how much more to pay.
  3. They upload proof for the extra amount and send the update.
  4. You open the order and see exactly what changed.
  5. Approve or reject it, and tap Mark payment received when the money arrives. Nothing changes until you say so.

Answers

Getting started

How do I set up my store fast?

Open the Store builder and pick what you sell: Beauty & hair, Fashion & accessories, Food & baking, Gadgets & electronics or Home & living. You get a design and sample products straight away. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Do I have to replace the sample products?

Yes. They are examples. Replace each one with your own photo, name and price. If you save while samples are still there, X Redro warns you first, because customers who open your link could order a sample.

Where do I put my bank details?

Replace the [brackets] in your store's sub heading with your bank name, account number and account name. Customers see it at the top of your store and in checkout, so they know where to pay.

How do I get my store link?

Open your dashboard and tap Copy store link. Put it in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp Status and DMs.

Is there a ready image and caption I can post?

Yes. On your dashboard tap Share kit. It makes a share image from your own store name, products, prices and pictures, in a tall size for WhatsApp Status or a square size for posts, plus three captions you can edit: WhatsApp Status, Instagram post and a reply to a customer. Replace any sample products before you share.

Can I change my store's design later?

Yes. In the Store builder, open Store design and choose any of the nine designs. Your products and settings stay.

Orders and payments

How do I know a customer has paid?

Every order arrives with the customer's payment proof attached. Check it against your bank, then mark the order Paid. You can also upload your bank statement in Verify payments and X Redro compares the proofs with the money that came in, flagging anything unclear for you to check.

What does payment verification need?

A text-based PDF bank statement. Scanned or photographed statements are not supported. X Redro reads the file in your browser and never uploads it or logs in to your bank.

A customer did not upload proof. What now?

The order shows that no proof was attached. Use the phone number on the order to ask them to send it, and keep the order Unpaid until you have confirmed the payment.

Can I delete a test order?

Yes. On the Orders page, use Delete orders and choose the order you want to remove.

Can I send customers to WhatsApp after they order?

Yes. In Settings, turn on "Send customers to WhatsApp after they submit an order" and enter your WhatsApp number.

When a customer wants to change an order

What is the order card?

After ordering, the customer downloads a card with a 12-character order ID. With the ID they can open Retrieve My Order on your store to see their order, add products or change quantities, and upload extra payment proof if they owe more.

Do I have to approve changes?

Yes. Every change waits for you to approve or reject it, and nothing is marked paid until you record the payment. The original order is kept, so you can always see what changed.

A customer lost their order card.

Open the order and tap Order card and ID. Ask the customer to tell you at least two things that match the order (for example the phone number or what they bought) without you reading the answers out. Then you can replace their ID and send them a new card.

Can I change an order for a customer?

Yes. Open the order and tap Change order for customer. You will be asked to check at least two things with the customer first. The change is recorded as made by you.

Can I turn this off?

Yes. In Settings you can switch off customer order changes. The Retrieve My Order bar then disappears from your store.

Products and stock

How do I stop customers ordering something that is gone?

In the Store builder, tap Mark sold out on that product. Customers see it straight away and cannot add it, and the rest of your store keeps selling.
