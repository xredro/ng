How to Sell on WhatsApp Status and Get More Orders

Updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

WhatsApp Status puts your products in front of people who already have your number. The trick is making it easy for them to order.

What to post

  • One clear photo or short video per product.
  • The price and available sizes or colours in the text.
  • A single line telling people how to order.
  • Proof: customer feedback, delivery photos, packing videos.

Keep Status posts tidy

Too many posts in one go makes people skip your whole set. Share your best items in a short batch rather than twenty at once.

Make ordering obvious

Many people view a Status and forget to reply. Put the way to order in the post itself, such as a link or a short instruction, so they do not have to message you first to find out.

Turn replies into orders

  1. Reply fast. Interest cools quickly.
  2. Send the same order template or link every time.
  3. Confirm the total, including delivery, before asking for payment.
  4. Log the order straight away.

Status disappears after 24 hours. Keep a record of what you sold and to whom somewhere permanent.

Grow your audience

Ask satisfied customers to save your number, and share your link in groups where it is allowed. A bigger saved-contact list means more views per post.
