How to Run a Thrift (Okrika) Business Online in Nigeria

Updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

Thrift is popular because every piece is different. That is also what makes it tricky to manage: each item can only be sold once.

Sourcing

Buy from sellers you trust and inspect what you can. Sort items by quality so you can price and describe them honestly.

Photograph every piece

  • Use good natural light and a clean background.
  • Show the front, back, label and any flaws.
  • Include measurements, since sizes vary between brands.

Avoid double-selling

When two customers want the same single item, the first confirmed order wins. Mark items as sold as soon as they are paid for, and tell the second customer promptly.

Price by item

List a price per piece, and consider bundle deals, such as three tops for a set price. Check your cost and profit first.

Run drops

  1. Announce a drop date and time.
  2. Post all items together with clear codes or numbers.
  3. Take orders through one route, first come first served.
  4. Close the drop and ship in batches.

Give every piece a code in the photo. "Item 12" is much faster to order than "the blue top in the third picture".
