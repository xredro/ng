How to Run a Pre-Order or Product Drop in Nigeria

Updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

Pre-orders let you sell before you stock. They also create a few days of heavy order traffic, which is where things tend to go wrong.

Set the rules before you announce

  • How long the pre-order window stays open
  • The delivery or arrival date you expect
  • Whether payment is full or a deposit
  • What happens if an item sells out or is delayed

Announce clearly

Show the products with prices, state the closing date and tell people exactly how to order. One clear route is better than three confusing ones.

Collect every order the same way

If some customers order by DM, some by comment and some by call, you will have three lists to merge. Use a single form or template for everyone.

Track as the window runs

  1. Log each order with the product, quantity and contact details.
  2. Mark payment status as it comes in.
  3. When the window closes, total up each product so you know how much to buy or import.
  4. Only place your supplier order for confirmed, paid items.

Count paid orders, not interested people. Ordering stock for unpaid enquiries is how pre-order sellers get stuck.

Keep customers updated

Message customers when stock arrives and when you ship. Fewer "where is my order?" messages means more time for the next drop.
