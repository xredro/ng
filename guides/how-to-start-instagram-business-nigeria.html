How to Start an Instagram Business in Nigeria

Updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

You do not need a shop or a big budget to sell on Instagram. You need a product people want, a clear page and a reliable way to take orders.

1. Pick something you can supply reliably

Start with one product category you can get consistently: clothes, bags, hair, perfume, snacks, phone accessories. A narrow focus makes your page easier to remember than a page that sells everything.

2. Check the numbers first

Know what the item costs you, what delivery costs, and what you will charge. Our guide on pricing and profit shows how.

3. Set up a page people trust

  • Use a clear name and profile photo.
  • Write in your bio what you sell, where you deliver and how to order.
  • Post clear, well-lit photos of the real product.
  • Save customer feedback and delivery proof into a highlight.

4. Decide how people order

Pick one route and say it everywhere. For example: "Tap the link in bio to order." Customers who have to guess how to buy usually do not.

5. Get your first ten orders

  1. Tell friends, family and contacts, and post on your WhatsApp Status.
  2. Post consistently, a few times a week.
  3. Reply to comments and DMs quickly.
  4. Deliver on time and ask happy customers for a review.

Your first customers are your marketing. One well-handled order often brings the next two.

6. Stay organised from day one

Record every order, its payment and its delivery status, even when you only have a few. Habits built early save you when sales pick up.
