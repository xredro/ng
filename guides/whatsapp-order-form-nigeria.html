How to Create a WhatsApp Order Form in Nigeria

Updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

An order form saves you from asking the same questions in every chat. A good one is short, clear and easy to finish on a phone.

What to put on the form

  • Product choice and quantity
  • Variants such as size or colour
  • Name and phone number
  • Delivery address
  • A place to upload payment proof, if you take transfers

What to leave out

Every extra question costs you completed orders. If you do not need a detail to fulfil the order, do not ask for it.

Where to build it

You can use a free form builder to start. They collect answers, but you will still be sorting responses yourself, and payment proof and order status are not built in. A tool made for orders keeps products, totals and payment status together.

Make it work on phones

Most customers will open your link from WhatsApp. Test it on your own phone: big buttons, short fields, nothing that needs zooming.

Share it where customers already are

  1. Paste the link in your WhatsApp Status with a clear call to action.
  2. Reply to enquiries with the link instead of typing out the details.
  3. Add it to your Instagram bio and TikTok profile.

Test your own form once a week. Place a dummy order to make sure nothing is broken before a big promotion.
