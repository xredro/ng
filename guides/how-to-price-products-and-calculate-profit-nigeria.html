How to Price Your Products and Calculate Profit

Updated 24 October 2026 · 4 min read

Many small sellers price by guessing and only find out later that delivery and small costs ate the profit. Here is a simple way to price properly.

Add up your real cost

  • What you paid for the item
  • Transport or delivery to you
  • Packaging
  • Delivery to the customer, if you pay it
  • Transfer or payment charges
  • Any ads or data you spent on that sale

A simple example

Example only: you buy a bag for ₦8,000, pay ₦1,500 to get it delivered to you and ₦300 for packaging. Your cost is ₦9,800. If you sell it for ₦12,000, your profit is ₦2,200.

To find your profit margin, divide profit by selling price: 2,200 ÷ 12,000 is about 18 percent.

Choose your price

  1. Work out total cost per item.
  2. Decide the profit you want per item.
  3. Add them to get your price.
  4. Compare with similar sellers. If you are far higher, make sure your photos, service or quality justify it.

Leave room for discounts. If you often bargain, build that margin into the price instead of cutting into your profit.

Delivery: charge it or absorb it?

State your delivery fee clearly before checkout. Surprise costs cause abandoned orders and arguments.

Review your numbers monthly

Prices from suppliers change. Check your costs regularly so you are not selling at a loss without noticing.
