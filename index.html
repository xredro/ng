Questions sellers ask

What is X Redro?

X Redro is an order management platform for Nigerian sellers who get orders on WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. You create an order form, share one link, and every order lands in one dashboard with its payment proof and status.

Do I need a website to use it?

No. X Redro gives you a shareable order link you can put in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp Status or broadcast. There is nothing to build or host, and no CAC registration is needed.

How does payment verification work?

Customers upload a screenshot of their transfer. You then upload your own bank statement as a text-based PDF, and X Redro compares the screenshots with the incoming credits on it. Anything unclear is flagged so you can check it yourself instead of guessing.

Does X Redro connect to my bank account?

No. It never logs in to your bank. You upload your statement yourself, so you stay in control of your banking details.

How much does X Redro cost?

You can start with a free 7-day trial. After that it is ₦1,000 for 7 days or ₦3,000 for 30 days.

Can customers order from their phones?

Yes. The order page is built for phones, since most of your customers will open your link from WhatsApp or Instagram.
