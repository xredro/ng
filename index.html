Questions sellers ask

What is X Redro?

X Redro lets Nigerian sellers create an online store without building a website. You choose a design, add your products, share one link on WhatsApp or Instagram, and every order arrives in one dashboard with its payment proof and status.

Do I need a website to use it?

No. X Redro gives you a store link to put in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp Status or broadcast. There is nothing to build or host, and no CAC registration is needed.

Can I choose how my store looks?

Yes. There are nine store designs. You can add your logo, a banner image, a short tagline, an about section and your contact details. Anything you leave blank is simply not shown.

How do customers pay?

Customers pay you by bank transfer, the way they already do, and upload a screenshot of the transfer with their order. X Redro does not process card payments.

How does payment verification work?

You upload your own bank statement as a text-based PDF, and X Redro compares customers' transfer screenshots with the incoming credits on it. Anything unclear is flagged so you can check it yourself instead of guessing.

Does X Redro connect to my bank account?

No. It never logs in to your bank. You upload your statement yourself, so you stay in control of your banking details.

How much does X Redro cost?

You can start with a free 7-day trial. After that it is ₦1,000 for 7 days or ₦3,000 for 30 days.

Can customers change their order after they send it?

Yes, if you leave this switched on in your settings. After ordering, a customer downloads an order card with an ID. They enter it on your store to see their order, add products or change quantities, and upload extra payment proof if they owe more. Every change waits for your approval, and you can recover or replace a lost ID from your Orders page.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Stores and the seller dashboard are designed for phones first, since most of your customers will open your link from WhatsApp or Instagram.
